Contact us

Questions, problems, ideas or a dive site we should add? Email onebreath1991@outlook.com. You can also use Account › Send feedback inside the app. We usually answer within two days.

In an emergency

Call your local emergency number first. In the app, the red SOS button shows the number for your country and first-aid steps. One Breath is a learning and training aid; it does not replace a freediving course with a certified instructor, a buddy, or medical advice. Never train breath-holds in water alone.

Common questions

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and open the link we send you on your iPhone.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Account › Delete account. Everything linked to your account is deleted permanently. See the privacy policy.

Someone posted something wrong or is bothering me.
Tap Report on the post, photo, profile or chat, and you can also Block the diver. We review reports within 24 hours and remove content that breaks the community rules.

How do I get a verified badge?
Upload a photo of your freediving certificate in your profile. A reviewer checks it and adds the badge. The photo stays private.

Does the app work without internet?
Yes: the manual, sea life, terms, dive sites, training, logbook, emergency numbers and forms all work offline. The last forecast and nearby-help list you opened for a spot are saved too. The community and new forecasts need a connection.

The forecast looks different from the sea.
Forecasts come from ocean and weather models, not live readings. Always look at the water yourself and ask local divers.
