Everything a freediver needs, in one app

  • Learn: a full freediving manual from your first breath-hold to instructor-level knowledge, safety and rescue, 53 sea creatures and 99 terms.
  • Train: breath-hold test, CO₂ and O₂ tables, a breathing guide and calm music.
  • Dive: 106 dive sites around the world with wind, waves, tides and a "Safe to dive?" check, plus the nearest hospital and coast guard.
  • Log: every discipline, personal bests and achievements.
  • Connect: dive photos, a forum, meet-ups, chat and a directory of instructors and schools.

Free on iPhone and iPad. Works offline.
