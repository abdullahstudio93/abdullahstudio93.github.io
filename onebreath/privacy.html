Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information One Breath: Freediving ("the app", "we") collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. The app is made by Abdullah Studio. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not use tracking or analytics tools.

What stays only on your phone

  • The freediving manual, sea life, terms, dive sites and forms are built into the app.
  • Your breath-hold timings, training tables, reading progress, checklist ticks and app settings.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: if you turn on the app lock, your face or fingerprint is checked by your iPhone itself. The app never receives or stores it.
  • Saved forecasts and saved lists of nearby help, kept so they work without signal.

Your account

To use One Breath you create an account with your email address and a password. When you sign up we store:

  • Your email address and a securely hashed password, to sign you in and to send password-reset emails.
  • Your role (fun diver, coach or instructor) and an optional display name.
  • Your dive log, saved spots, personal bests and settings, so they are backed up and appear on your other devices.

The community

If you use the community, the things you choose to share are stored so other divers can see them:

  • Profile: name, photo, bio, role, certification level, freediving since, personal bests and achievements. You can hide your profile from the community at any time with the "Show me in the Community" switch.
  • Birth date: kept private. Other divers see only your age, and only if you allow it.
  • Dive photos, likes, comments, forum posts, meet-ups and directory listings: visible to other signed-in divers.
  • Messages: visible only to you and the diver you write to. If you share a location in a chat or a meet-up, that one point is shared. The app never tracks your location in the background.
  • Certificates: the photo of a certificate you upload is private. Only you and the app's reviewers see it, to check it and give you a "verified" badge. Other divers see only the agency and level.
  • Follows, blocks and reports you make, so the app can apply them.

We do not read your messages. Because we run the service, stored content can technically be accessed by us; we only look at content when it is reported to us or when the law requires it.

Your location

  • Location is used only when you tap a button such as "My location" or "Share my location", to show the sea and the nearest help around you. It is not collected in the background.
  • To show sea conditions, map tiles and nearby hospitals, the coordinates of the place you are looking at are sent to the services below. They receive the place, not who you are.

Services we use

  • Supabase (servers in Singapore): stores accounts, profiles, the community and your backed-up data. Supabase privacy policy.
  • Open-Meteo: wind, waves, tides and weather forecasts for the place you check. Open-Meteo terms.
  • OpenStreetMap: map pictures, and the list of nearby hospitals and coast guard stations. OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
  • Apple: Apple Maps opens when you ask for directions or nearby help, and the App Store rating prompt is shown by iOS.

Who can use One Breath

One Breath is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping, changing and deleting your information

  • You can change your profile, delete photos, posts and certificates, and hide your profile in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account: in the app go to Account › Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, dive log, photos, certificates, posts, comments, messages and other data linked to it.
  • Signing out removes your data from that phone; it stays in your account until you delete it.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing us.

Security

Information travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access rules on our database let each diver read and change only what they are allowed to. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use elsewhere.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions or requests: onebreath1991@outlook.com.
